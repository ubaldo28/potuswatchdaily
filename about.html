About POTUS Watch Daily

Est. April 2026

POTUS Watch Daily is an AI-powered foreign policy intelligence publication dedicated to tracking and analyzing the global moves of the President of the United States.

Our Mission

We believe that understanding American foreign policy is essential for anyone who wants to make sense of the world. Our mission is to deliver sharp, timely, and honest analysis of every major move the White House makes on the global stage.

How We Work

POTUS Watch Daily combines real-time news monitoring with advanced artificial intelligence to produce analytical dispatches covering key regions including Iran, China, NATO, the Americas, the Middle East, Russia, and global trade. Our AI correspondent reads the latest news and produces in-depth analysis every hour, 24 hours a day.

Editorial Standards

All analysis published on POTUS Watch Daily is clearly labeled as AI-generated commentary and analysis. We source our news from reputable international news organizations and clearly cite our sources in every dispatch.
