About

Built by someone who got tired of being lied to.

POTUS Watch Daily started as a question: what would foreign policy coverage look like if it had no editorial agenda, no corporate sponsor, and no side to take?

Every major outlet covers geopolitics through a lens — left, right, establishment, contrarian. The framing is baked in before the reporter types a word. After years of reading between the lines, trying to piece together what was actually happening in the world versus what various outlets wanted you to think was happening, the frustration became unbearable.

So this site was built. Not by a newsroom. Not by a media company. By one person with a deep interest in how power actually moves in the world — and a belief that most people, regardless of politics, just want a clear-eyed account of events.

The model is simple: real news sources, independent analysis, no spin. Every hour, the latest foreign policy developments are monitored, synthesized through the lens of American strategic interests, and published as a dispatch. The voice is deliberately neutral. The goal is clarity, not confirmation.

We cover eight regions: Americas, China, NATO, Iran, the Middle East, Russia, Trade, and in-depth Analysis. Each dispatch draws on verified news sources and is written to inform — not to persuade.

POTUS Watch Daily is entirely independent. There are no investors, no editorial board, no advertisers with veto power over what gets covered. Running it costs money — servers, infrastructure, data sources — and it comes out of one person's pocket.
