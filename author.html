Our Team

POTUS Watch Daily · Washington D.C.

POTUS Watch Daily is an independent foreign policy intelligence publication tracking U.S. diplomacy, trade strategy, and geopolitical developments across eight global regions. Our dispatches are published around the clock, drawing on breaking news and primary source analysis.

James Rourke
Senior Foreign Policy Correspondent

James covers the White House, State Department, and Pentagon with a focus on strategic decision-making and the interplay between domestic politics and foreign policy. He has tracked U.S. foreign policy across three administrations, with particular expertise in the Middle East portfolio, sanctions policy, and NATO dynamics.

Catherine Lowe
Asia-Pacific & Trade Analyst

Catherine specialises in U.S.-China relations, Indo-Pacific strategy, and the geopolitics of global trade. Her analysis focuses on the economic leverage points that shape diplomatic outcomes — tariffs, supply chains, technology restrictions, and currency policy.

Marcus Hale
Eurasia & Security Correspondent

Marcus covers Russia, Ukraine, and the broader European security landscape. His reporting focuses on military strategy, alliance cohesion, and the long-term structural shifts reshaping the transatlantic relationship in the wake of Russia's invasion of Ukraine.
