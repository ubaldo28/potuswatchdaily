Transparency

Editorial Standards

POTUS Watch Daily is committed to accuracy, transparency, and editorial independence. This page explains how we source, verify, and publish our foreign policy coverage.

Our Mission

We exist to provide clear-eyed, analytical coverage of U.S. foreign policy — with no corporate sponsor, no political affiliation, and no editorial agenda. Our coverage focuses on policy, diplomacy, economics, and strategy across eight global regions: Americas, China, NATO, Iran, the Middle East, Russia, Trade, and Analysis.

Sourcing Standards

Every dispatch published on POTUS Watch Daily draws on verified news sources. We only reference reporting from established news organisations, government statements, official press releases, and credible policy institutions. Sources are cited at the bottom of every article so readers can verify claims independently.

  • We do not publish rumour, speculation, or unverified claims
  • All source links are live and traceable at time of publication
  • We cross-reference multiple sources before making analytical claims
  • Government and official statements are treated as primary sources

Editorial Independence

POTUS Watch Daily has no investors, no editorial board, and no advertisers with influence over our coverage. Advertising on this site is served through third-party networks and has no bearing on what we publish or how we frame it. Our analysis reflects an independent assessment of publicly available information.

Coverage of Armed Conflict

POTUS Watch Daily does not condone, glorify, dismiss, or exploit armed conflict of any kind. Our coverage of wars — including Russia's ongoing war of aggression in Ukraine — is strictly analytical and policy-focused. We report on conflict as a geopolitical reality that shapes diplomacy and strategy. We do not sensationalise violence or benefit commercially from conflict coverage.

Analytical Framework

Our analysis is written through the lens of American strategic interests — meaning we assess how events affect U.S. foreign policy objectives, alliance relationships, and global standing. This is a framing device, not an ideological position. We cover administrations of all political stripes with the same analytical approach.

Corrections Policy

We take accuracy seriously. If we publish incorrect information, we correct it promptly and transparently. Corrections are noted within the relevant article. If you believe we have published something factually inaccurate, please contact us at potuswatchdaily@gmail.com with the specific claim and supporting evidence.

Affiliate Relationships

Some articles on this site contain affiliate links. When a reader makes a purchase through an affiliate link, POTUS Watch Daily may earn a small commission at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate relationships have no influence on our editorial content or the topics we cover. All affiliate links are clearly disclosed within the relevant article.

Advertising

This site displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense and potentially other ad networks. Advertisers have no editorial influence over our content. Ad content is the responsibility of the respective advertiser. We do not endorse products or services advertised on this site unless explicitly stated.

Contact the Editor

Questions about our editorial standards, sourcing decisions, or corrections can be directed to potuswatchdaily@gmail.com. We read every message and respond to substantive editorial queries within 48 hours.
